# Privacy Policy

**Product:** Crypto Volume Viewer (`cryptovol`)  
**Operator:** Tilmann Felgner  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-29

Crypto Volume Viewer is a local, read-only tool for inspecting and extracting files from encrypted volume/container files on your computer.

## What the app does

You choose an encrypted container file, enter the password you already have, browse the decrypted filesystem in memory, and optionally extract a single file to a destination you name. The source container is never modified, mounted, or written.

## Data collection

The app does **not** collect, transmit, store, or sell personal data.

- No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
- No accounts, cloud sync, or advertising identifiers.
- No network requests of its own. There is no auto-update check and no background process.
- Passwords, derived keys, and decrypted contents stay on your machine. They are not logged and are not sent anywhere.

## Files on your computer

The only files the app reads are the container you open and, during extraction, the destination you choose. Extracted files are unencrypted copies of content from the container. You are responsible for where those copies go and when they are deleted.
